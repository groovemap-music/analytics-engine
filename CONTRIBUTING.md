# Contributing

External contributions are temporarily paused. Please do not open pull requests or submit patches to this repository while this gate is in place. Issue reports and technical discussion remain welcome.

GrooveMap intends to offer both AGPL and separately negotiated commercial terms. Before accepting external contributions, the project needs an approved relicensing-capable Contributor License Agreement that gives the project the rights needed to distribute contributed work under both licensing models while preserving contributor attribution and rights.

This policy will be updated when that Contributor License Agreement and its acceptance process are approved. Until then, maintainers will not merge externally authored changes.
